2025 Congressional Districts

These Congressional districts were established by the voters of California with the passage of Proposition 50 (2025) on November 4, 2025 and will go into effect after the November 2026 elections and upon seating of the 120th Congress, January 3, 2027. The districts will stay in use until the California Citizens Redistricting Commission draws new maps following the 2030 U.S. Census.
