       SPRINGFIELD (July 29, 2022) – The Illinois State Board of Elections today certified the results
of the June 28 primary election, in which voters nominated candidates from established parties for the
Nov. 8 general election.
       The official data shows 1,757,872 voters out of a total of 8.1 million registered voters cast ballots
in the primary for a turnout of 21.68 percent. That figure is the second lowest for a primary election in
the last 40 years, higher only than the 18.09 percent turnout in 2014 (See chart below.)
       With 911,919 ballots cast, Democrats made up 51.88 percent of the total vote. Republican ballots
totaled 812,133 and 46.2 percent of the total. The Libertarian Party is established for certain offices in
five counties, accounting for 2,425 votes, and 41,756 voters statewide cast non-partisan ballots for local
propositions only.

       The State Board of Elections currently is adjudicating objections to candidates who were slated
to fill vacancies in which no candidate was nominated at the primary and to independent and new party
candidates who filed their nominating petitions in July. The board is scheduled to certify the ballot for
the Nov. 8 General Election on Aug. 26.
