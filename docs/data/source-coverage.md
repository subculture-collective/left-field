# Source coverage

## Meaning and scope

This is an evidence inventory, not a claim that every locked item is published. A source-lock entry is byte-identified input, extract, artifact, contract, or planning material; `retained` means its recorded path is in the repository, while `nonretained` means only its identity, size, and hash are locked. The current lock has **108 entries: 101 retained and 7 nonretained**. The seven nonretained entries are three ACS raw tables and four FEC responses. Verify it with `npm run data:verify` ([`data/source-lock.json`](../../data/source-lock.json)).

Coverage records use an explicit denominator and status (`complete`, `partial`, or an explicit missing/unavailable judgment) for a named release and domain; they do not turn a source lock into publication. `candidate-only` means a disposable or unpublished candidate was validated and is not public. `published` below means the R1 identity/geography release only. Missing credentials, a landing page, unreviewed material, or an absent lock entry means `not_collected` or `unassessed` as applicable—not `source_unavailable` or `license_unavailable`. Those reasons require a versioned assessment.
